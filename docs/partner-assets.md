# Partner Section Assets

The four transparent SVGs in `public/images/partners/` are generic healthcare illustrations, not partner-company logos. The official site does not publish partner names in this section, so they are not presented as verified organizational endorsements.

All four assets are by Javad Samiee from the [Medical Line Vectors collection](https://www.svgrepo.com/collection/medical-line-vectors/) on SVG Repo and are listed under its [CC Attribution license](https://www.svgrepo.com/page/licensing/#CC%20Attribution). Their stroke width was increased and their fill/stroke colors were changed to the footer gradient.
